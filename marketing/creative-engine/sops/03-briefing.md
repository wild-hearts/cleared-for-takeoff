# SOP 03: Briefing

**Purpose:** turn an approved ad concept into something a creator can execute.
One brief per concept, written from the template below. The creator might be
me, might be the AI crew, might one day be a freelancer; the brief is written
so all three would produce roughly the same asset. If the output depends on who
made it, the brief was a suggestion, not a brief.

## Rules

1. **One brief per concept.** A brief covering two concepts produces an asset
   that tests neither.
2. **The brief contains everything Production needs and nothing else.** Every
   question Production has to come back and ask is a defect in this SOP, not in
   Production.
3. **Copy the concept card in, don't reference it.** Briefs get executed at
   odd hours in separate tools. A brief that says "see concept card" will be
   executed from memory.
4. **Voice is specified, not assumed.** Every brief names the voice rules
   below, because the person or model producing the asset may not know them.

## The template

Save as `../briefs/BRIEF-[concept-short-name].md`.

```
# Brief: [concept short name]

## From the concept card (copied, not linked)
micropersona:  ...
awareness:     ...
anchor:        ... (include the verbatim)
hook:          ...
mechanic:      ...
hypothesis:    ...

## Deliverable
format:        [pin 1000x1500 | fb image 1080x1080 | vertical video 1080x1920 ≤60s | share card 1200x630 | email]
count:         [n variants, and what varies: hook wording, image, first 3 seconds]
destination:   [where it will run, and the landing URL]

## What it must say
- The offer facts, verbatim from research/product-facts.md: [only the ones this ad uses]
- The one action we want: [start Module 1 free | read the article | join the waitlist]

## What it must never say
- No income, side-hustle, or productivity-multiplier claims. Ever.
- No claims about features that do not exist yet (check product-facts.md).
- Nothing that talks down to the reader. "You are not behind" is the brand.

## Voice
- Australian English, plain words, warm, no hype.
- No em dashes. No "unlock", "elevate", "game-changer", "journey", "seamless".
- Reads like Naomi wrote it at the kitchen table, because notionally she did.

## Craft notes
[anything visual or structural: colours from styles.css, the aviation motif,
reference asset if one exists]

status: drafted | in production | produced
```

## Done when

The brief answers every question Production could ask, the facts section has
been checked against `product-facts.md` on the day of writing (facts change;
briefs go stale), and status is set. Then it goes to Production (SOP 04)
untouched.
