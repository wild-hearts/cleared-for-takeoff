# SOP 01: Research

**Purpose:** know the customer and the product before having ideas. Raw data
lands untouched, gets processed through this SOP, and becomes everything the
engine consumes. Ideation never invents a pain point; it withdraws one from the
vault.

## The two vaults

**Product vault** (`../research/product-facts.md`): the product catalogue with
features, facts only. No adjectives, no benefits, no positioning. "Six modules,
roughly 6 to 8 hours of video, $49 AUD once, Module 1 free, lifetime access,
quarterly updates" is a fact. "Life-changing" is not, and it does not go in this
file. The vault also records what does **not** exist yet (course videos pending,
workbook pending), because the QA SOP checks claims against this file and "only
advertise what exists" is rule five of the engine.

**Customer vault** (`../research/customer-voice/`): what real people say, in
their own words. One file per source.

## Sources, in priority order

1. **Amazon reviews** of beginner AI books (ours and competitors'). Reviews of
   competitors are a free focus group that someone else paid to run.
2. **Comments and replies** on our own social posts and blog articles.
3. **Emails and contact-form messages** from readers and students, especially
   the confused ones. A confused email is a hook that has not been written yet.
4. **Facebook groups and Reddit threads** where beginners ask AI questions
   (over-50s groups, small business groups, r/ArtificialInteligence beginner
   threads). Capture the question as asked, not our summary of it.
5. **Course and free-lesson feedback**, once there is enough volume to matter.

## Capture rules

- **Verbatim or nothing.** Copy the exact words, typos included. The moment a
  quote gets paraphrased it stops being research and becomes what we already
  believed, wearing a wig.
- **Tag every entry** with source, URL if there is one, date captured, and one
  or more segment tags matching the site's audience pages (trades,
  small-business, professionals, later-life, creators, education, health,
  nonprofits, hospitality-retail, property, parents).
- **Tag the anchor**: is this a pain (fear, confusion, feeling behind, scam
  worry) or a desire (keep up with grandkids, save admin time, win back
  evenings)? One word: `pain` or `desire`.
- **No editorial at capture.** Analysis happens in Ideation, on purpose, with a
  goal in hand. Capture is dumb by design.

## Entry format

```
> "I'm 67 and everyone assumes I already know what a chatbot is. I don't."
source: Amazon review, [competitor title], 3 stars
url: ...
captured: 2026-08-20
segment: later-life
anchor: pain
```

## Cadence

Thirty minutes weekly, calendared, not "when I get to it". Findings from the
Analysis SOP (08) also land here, in `../research/findings.md`, which is the
vault's third shelf: things we have proven true or false with our own launches.

## Done when

Each session ends with new verbatims filed, tagged, and dated. If a session
finds nothing new, write that down too, with where you looked, so the next
session looks somewhere else.
