# Campaign brief — The Momentum Rule, 90 days

For Archer (plan), Red (words), Ember (the scheduled runs) and Bench (review).
Full reasoning in `research/momentum-rule-90-day-plan.md`.

## What this is

Built on the 95/5 hook-modelling method from a TikTok by @theinfinitekid: pick one
platform, search your book's keywords, like what comes back to prime the algorithm,
save the top five videos over 100k views, keep 95 per cent of a proven hook and
change 5 per cent, film it, post to all four short-form platforms, warm new
accounts first, run it for 30, 60, 90 days.

**That method is a views strategy, not a sales strategy.** Nowhere in its seven
steps does a viewer become a buyer, and Amazon never comes up. Both gaps are closed
here. If an agent proposes work that generates views without touching the
conversion path or the listing, that is the method's blind spot reappearing.

## The conversion path

- **Default, every day** → `themomentumrule.com/start`. Email first, then both buy
  buttons, direct listed first.
- **Rank push, five days only** → Amazon direct. Concentration is the mechanism:
  fifty sales in five days moves category rank, the same fifty over a month moves
  nothing.
- **Anyone already on the list** → Amazon direct. Their email is already held, so
  spend the click on rank.

Four short links, one per platform, all landing on `/start` with a different tag.
Without them there is no way to tell which platform sends buyers rather than
viewers, and the 90-day review has nothing to decide on.

## The amendment to 95/5

Copy the **structure** of a hook, never the sentence. A hook works because of its
shape: a named enemy, a contradiction, a specific mundane detail, an unfinished
thought. The words are the product, and this brand is explicitly anti-guru, so a
feed of recycled guru phrasing contradicts the book on sight.

**Red:** when adapting a harvested hook, take the skeleton and write the flesh in
the brand voice. Bench should reject anything that reads like the source video.

## Content pillars

| Day | Pillar | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | The Enemy | Motivation, readiness, the perfect time, "when I..." statements. Names a villain the viewer already resents. |
| Tue | The Trenches | Grief, exhaustion, caregiving, slow collapse. Validates the freeze without coddling it, then moves. |
| Wed | The Evidence | The smallest possible step. Confidence arriving after the action. |
| Thu | The Songs | A Winks track as original audio. |
| Fri | Best of week | Re-cut whichever performed best, different hook. |

**The songs are the moat.** Every hook-copier is fighting over the same trending
audio. Uploading original tracks means a video nobody can replicate, a second
discovery surface when someone finds the song first, and a royalty stream that pays
whether or not the book sells. Start with the titles that are already hooks: *Even
Like That*, *Running On Fumes*, *The Waiting Room*, *But I Will*, *The Blooper
Reel*, *Wet Cement*, *The Minimum Requirement*.

## Shooting formats

Rotate roughly 3 : 1 : 1 per week.

1. **Talking head.** 7 to 15 seconds, hook straight to camera, wherever she is. The
   reference video was shot in a parked car.
2. **B-roll and text.** Atmospheric clip with the hook as on-screen text.
3. **The multimodal demo.** Book open, scan through to the site, play the companion
   track. The only format that answers what makes this book different.

## Twenty hooks, in the brand voice

Grouped by the structure each one borrows. One per video, said in the first 1.5
seconds, then 25 seconds earning it.

**Named enemy**
1. Motivation is a con artist. It turns up in week one with champagne and sends a postcard from Bali in week three.
2. Confidence is not a personality type. It is evidence your body collected while you were not looking.
3. You are not lazy. You are waiting for permission from someone who does not exist.
4. Week three does not come with a speech. It just comes with the work, sitting there, requiring you again.

**The specific mundane detail**
5. The running shoes have been in the hallway so long the dog has started sleeping on them.
6. Your project folder sits on the desktop like a disappointed parent who has chosen not to say anything.
7. The app says last opened fourteen days ago, and your stomach does a small guilty lurch.
8. Momentum does not slam the door. It is a phone on two per cent swearing it is fine, then dying mid-sentence.

**The number that reframes**
9. Zero is the only number that kills momentum. One badly done rep does not.
10. I wrote twelve books before I released one. The thirteenth went first, because it explained how I survived the other twelve.
11. Two legs and a heartbeat is enough.
12. Sixty days. One small thing a day. That is the entire method, and it is why it works.

**Permission removed**
13. Nobody is coming to assess you. You have been standing at an empty desk for three years.
14. Stop waiting until you know more. You will not know more, you will just be older and still waiting.
15. The invitation is not coming. The universe is busy making sure your phone dies when you need directions.
16. There is no one with the authority to tell you that you are allowed to keep going. There never was.

**Straight into the trench**
17. If getting out of bed feels like climbing Everest in flip-flops, this one is for you.
18. You are functioning while exhausted and thinking clearly while carrying too much. I am not about to tell you to do less.
19. A smaller version of the thing still counts. That is not a consolation prize, it is the whole method.
20. Most people do not lose momentum because they failed. They lose it because they vanished quietly, and nobody called.

## The one number

In month one, saves per 1,000 views carries more information than the rest
combined. A like is a reflex. A save is someone deciding they want this again
later, which for a book is the closest free signal to intent to buy that any
platform gives you.

## Phase gates

Do not advance a phase because the calendar says so. Advance when the gate clears.

- **W0 → P1:** accounts warmed three days, `/start` live, four short links tested on
  a phone, ten videos filmed and exported.
- **P1 → P2:** twenty videos posted, four full weeks of metrics, top three hooks
  and the losing pillar identified.
- **P2 → P3:** rank push executed, its result recorded against the pre-push
  baseline.
- **P3 → next quarter:** the 90-day review answers whether short-form sent buyers
  or only viewers, using short-link and Attribution data, not vibes.
