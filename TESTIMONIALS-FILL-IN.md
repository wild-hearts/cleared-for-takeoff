The site is now built and waiting for these. The CSS is in, the schema is written, the
two components are ready. The moment you paste real quotes into this file, wiring them

I have deliberately not written placeholder quotes. Invented testimonials are a breach of

## What makes a quote work

The weak ones are general praise. The strong ones name the fear before the fix.

- **Weak:** "Great course, learned a lot, highly recommend."
- **Strong:** "I had been nodding along in meetings for a year pretending I knew what
  ChatGPT was. Module 1 took ninety minutes and I stopped pretending."

Ask for the second kind by asking the right question. Do not ask "did you enjoy the course".
Ask these three:

1. What were you worried about before you started?
2. What can you do now that you could not do a month ago?
3. What would you say to someone your age who thinks it is too late?

Answer three is usually the testimonial.

## If you do not have six students yet

You almost certainly do not, and that is fine. Do not wait, and do not fake it. In order of
how fast they can be gathered:

1. **Book readers.** *Cleared For Take-Off* is live on Amazon, Apple Books, Kobo and Google
   Play. Anyone who has read it and acted on it can speak to your method. Label the quote
   honestly as a reader, not a student.
2. **Consulting clients.** If anyone has been through the AI Readiness Assessment or a
   tailored training session, they are your strongest proof because they paid more.
3. **Run a free beta cohort.** Give Modules 1 and 2 free to twelve people from your list in
   exchange for an honest quote, agreed up front. You will have six usable quotes inside a
   fortnight and some real feedback on the course. This is the fastest legitimate route.
4. **Your own credibility, stated plainly.** Until quotes exist, the honest fallback is a
   founder line: former aviation manager, the book, the method. Not as strong as a student
   voice, but it is true, and an empty space is worse than either.

If you take route 3, note in the quote that they received free access. The ACCC requires
material connections to be disclosed. One clause does it: "(received free access in
exchange for honest feedback)".
