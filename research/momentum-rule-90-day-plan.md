# Ninety Days of Momentum

A short-form video and Amazon plan for *The Momentum Rule*, built on the method in
the @theinfinitekid TikTok, with the parts it leaves out filled in.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *The Momentum Rule: Stop Waiting. Start Moving. Even Like That.* |
| **Position** | Book 1 of 3, The Momentum Series |
| **Selling now** | Amazon Kindle · themomentumrule.com |

---

## 01 · What the video actually says

The creator claims zero to 150,000 books sold and a couple of number one
bestsellers, and says he was a college dropout. Treat the numbers as a claim, not
a fact. The method is seven steps.

1. **Pick one platform.** TikTok or Instagram. Not both. One.
2. **Search your keywords.** Put words related to your book into the search bar.
   His definition: "anything in your title, just words that are key to your book."
3. **Like what comes back.** Liking primes the algorithm so your feed fills with
   videos from your book's niche. In his words, "that's what an algorithm is."
4. **Save the top five viral videos.** Viral means over 100,000 views, ideally
   over a million.
5. **95/5 the hook.** Keep 95 per cent of the hook the same, change 5 per cent so
   it carries your own vibe. Framed as Tony Robbins style modelling: copy the
   behaviour of people who already succeeded to get there faster.
6. **Open the camera app and film.** Record yourself delivering those hooks. If
   you will not put your face on camera, he names software you feed a manuscript
   and a book video to, which returns a 95/5 video for you.
7. **Post to all four, then stay consistent.** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube,
   Facebook. Warm new accounts first by using them like a normal person for a
   couple of days, otherwise you sit at zero views. Then run it for 30, 60, 90 days.

## 02 · The honest read

Four things in it are right and worth doing exactly as described.

- **Account warming is real and almost everyone skips it.** A brand new account
  that posts immediately gets throttled. Three days of behaving like a human first
  is the cheapest win in the whole method.
- **Hook modelling works.** The first 1.5 seconds decide everything downstream.
  Studying hooks that already earned a million views beats inventing your own from
  a standing start.
- **One shoot, four platforms.** Filming once and posting to TikTok, Reels, Shorts
  and Facebook is free distribution. There is no reason not to.
- **Ninety days before judging.** This is the actual mechanism, and it is the step
  most people quit at. Week three has no champagne in it.

Two things are missing, and they are the two that decide whether any of this turns
into sales.

- **It is a views plan, not a sales plan.** Nowhere in the seven steps does a
  viewer become a buyer. Nobody sold 150,000 books on views alone. They sold them
  on views plus a path, and the path is the part he does not show. Section 03
  builds it.
- **Amazon does not appear at all.** Once a listing has sales velocity, reviews and
  correct categories, Amazon's own algorithm will outsell your social output and
  keep doing it while you sleep. Section 08 covers that half.

### One amendment to step 5

Copy the *structure* of a hook, not the sentence. A hook works because of its
shape: a named enemy, a contradiction, a specific mundane detail, an unfinished
thought. That shape is worth taking. The words are not, because on a self-help
account the words are the product. *The Momentum Rule* is explicitly anti-guru, and
a feed of recycled guru phrasing contradicts the book on sight. Take the skeleton
at 95 per cent. Write the flesh yourself. Section 06 has twenty already written.

> **The software he names.** At roughly 1:50 he names a tool that turns a
> manuscript plus a book video into a finished 95/5 video. Auto-captions render it
> as "Saunder", which is almost certainly wrong. Check his profile or comments for
> the real name before paying for anything, and be aware that a tool which
> generates your hooks for you defeats the amendment above.

## 03 · Build the path before you post anything

You are selling in two places at once, and they pull in opposite directions.
Amazon converts strangers well and feeds its own ranking machine, but you never
learn who bought. Your own site pays a far better margin and hands you an email
address, which is the only audience nobody can take away from you.

So do not pick one. Sequence them.

| Situation | Link goes to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Default, every day | themomentumrule.com/start | Captures the email first, then offers both buy buttons. You own the list. |
| Rank push, 5 days only | Amazon, directly | Concentrated sales in a short window move category rank. A steady trickle does not. |
| Anyone already on the list | Amazon, directly | You already have their email. Spend their click on rank instead. |

### The one page everything points at

Build **themomentumrule.com/start** and nothing else this week. Five blocks, in
this order.

- [ ] The Trenches paragraph, verbatim from the book. It is the strongest thing in
      the manuscript and it qualifies the reader in about eight seconds.
- [ ] Day 1 of the sixty day programme, free, with its song. Give the whole thing away.
- [ ] Email capture, one field, framed as "the next 59 days".
- [ ] Two buttons: Kindle on Amazon, and buy direct. Direct listed first.
- [ ] The three book series strip at the bottom, so a first-time reader learns
      there are two more before they have even bought one.

### Know which platform actually sells

Four short links, one per platform, all landing on the same page with a different
tag: `/tt`, `/ig`, `/yt`, `/fb`. Without this you will spend ninety days unable to
answer the only question that matters, which is where the buyers came from. For
the Amazon side, set up Amazon Attribution through your KDP or Ads account if it is
offered in your marketplace, since it is the only way to see off-Amazon clicks turn
into Amazon orders.

## 04 · Week zero, before a single video

Seven days of setup. Nothing gets posted in this week, which will feel wrong and is
correct.

### Days 1 to 3 · Warm the accounts

- [ ] Create or clean up four accounts: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook. Same
      handle everywhere if you can get it.
- [ ] On each, for 15 to 20 minutes a day: scroll, watch things all the way to the
      end, like 10 to 15, comment properly on 3, follow 5 accounts in the niche.
- [ ] Do it on the phone, on normal home wifi or mobile data. No VPN, no desktop emulator.
- [ ] Bio: one line of position and one link. Not a link tree with nine options.

### Days 1 to 3 · Prime the algorithm at the same time

This is steps 2 and 3 from the video, and it doubles as your warming activity.
Search these, watch what comes back, like the good ones, save anything over 100,000
views into a folder.

`momentum over motivation` · `stop waiting to feel ready` ·
`self help for people who hate self help` · `anti guru` ·
`discipline over motivation` · `functioning while exhausted` ·
`burnout single mum` · `caregiver burnout` ·
`grief and getting through the day` · `confidence comes after action` ·
`executive dysfunction motivation` · `micro habits when depressed` ·
`how to start when you have no motivation` · `rebuilding after divorce` ·
`booktok self help` · `books that actually helped me` · `60 day challenge` ·
`but I will`

### Days 4 to 5 · Build the path

- [ ] Ship `/start`. Five blocks, as above.
- [ ] Four tracked short links live and tested on a phone.
- [ ] Amazon Attribution set up if available.

### Days 6 to 7 · Batch film the first ten

- [ ] Ten hooks from section 06. One hour. Same clothes are fine, nobody is checking.
- [ ] Vertical, phone camera, natural light. The reference video is filmed in a
      parked car, which is the point.
- [ ] Captions burned in. Hook lands inside 1.5 seconds. Total length 25 to 40 seconds.

## 05 · The content system

One video a day, five days a week, posted to all four platforms. Twenty videos a
month, eighty pieces of distribution. Batch film ten at a time, twice a month.

Four pillars, one per posting day, and the fourth is the one nobody else can copy.

| Day | Pillar | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **The Enemy** | Motivation, readiness, the perfect time, "when I..." statements. Names a villain the viewer already resents. |
| Tue | **The Trenches** | Grief, exhaustion, caregiving, the slow collapse of something. Validates the freeze without coddling it, then moves. |
| Wed | **The Evidence** | The smallest possible step. Confidence arriving after the action, not before. Proof, not pep talk. |
| Thu | **The Songs** | A Winks track as the audio. See below. |
| Fri | *Best of the week* | Re-cut whichever of the four performed best, with a different hook. |

### The unfair advantage

There are fifty original songs by The Winks tied to the sixty day programme,
sitting on Spotify. Every hook-copier on TikTok is fighting over the same trending
audio. You can upload your own and be the only person on the platform using it.
That gives you three things at once: a video nobody can replicate, a second
discovery surface when someone finds the song first, and a royalty stream that pays
whether or not the book sells.

Start with the ones whose titles are already hooks: *Even Like That*, *Running On
Fumes*, *The Waiting Room*, *But I Will*, *The Blooper Reel*, *Wet Cement*, *The
Minimum Requirement*.

The sixty day app is the second advantage. Fifty-plus daily prompts already
written, a tracker, and five daily rules. Every one of those prompts is a video.
You have roughly three months of content sitting in a repository right now, which
means the content problem is solved and only the filming problem remains.

### Three ways to shoot it

Rotate these so the feed does not turn into ninety identical talking heads. The
third one is the only format on this list your competitors cannot copy.

| Format | How | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Talking head** | 7 to 15 seconds, hook straight to camera, wherever you happen to be. The reference video was shot in a parked car. | The Enemy and Permission Removed hooks, which need a human face to land |
| **B-roll and text** | Atmospheric clip (hands on a coffee cup, walking out a door, the running shoes in the hallway) with the hook as on-screen text. | The mundane-detail hooks, and any day you cannot face being on camera |
| **The multimodal demo** | Film the book open, scan through to the site, then play the companion track or conversation on screen. Show what a chapter with a song attached actually is. | Explaining the thing no other self-help book on the platform can offer |

Weight it roughly 3 talking head, 1 b-roll, 1 demo per week. The demo format is
slower to make and worth it, because it is the only one that answers the question a
browsing reader actually has, which is what makes this book different from the
forty others in their feed.

## 06 · Twenty hooks, already in your voice

Grouped by the structure they borrow. Use one per video, say it straight to camera
in the first 1.5 seconds, then spend 25 seconds earning it.

### Named enemy
*Borrowed shape: personify the thing the viewer already resents, then indict it.*

1. Motivation is a con artist. It turns up in week one with champagne and sends a postcard from Bali in week three.
2. Confidence is not a personality type. It is evidence your body collected while you were not looking.
3. You are not lazy. You are waiting for permission from someone who does not exist.
4. Week three does not come with a speech. It just comes with the work, sitting there, requiring you again.

### The specific mundane detail
*Borrowed shape: one concrete household image doing the whole argument. Strongest performer for saves.*

5. The running shoes have been in the hallway so long the dog has started sleeping on them.
6. Your project folder sits on the desktop like a disappointed parent who has chosen not to say anything.
7. The app says last opened fourteen days ago, and your stomach does a small guilty lurch.
8. Momentum does not slam the door. It is a phone on two per cent swearing it is fine, then dying mid-sentence.

### The number that reframes
*Borrowed shape: a hard figure in the first three words. Highest completion rate of the five.*

9. Zero is the only number that kills momentum. One badly done rep does not.
10. I wrote twelve books before I released one. The thirteenth went first, because it explained how I survived the other twelve.
11. Two legs and a heartbeat is enough.
12. Sixty days. One small thing a day. That is the entire method, and it is why it works.

### Permission removed
*Borrowed shape: name the thing the viewer is secretly waiting for, then confirm it is not coming.*

13. Nobody is coming to assess you. You have been standing at an empty desk for three years.
14. Stop waiting until you know more. You will not know more, you will just be older and still waiting.
15. The invitation is not coming. The universe is busy making sure your phone dies when you need directions.
16. There is no one with the authority to tell you that you are allowed to keep going. There never was.

### Straight into the trench
*Borrowed shape: address one specific reader state so precisely that everyone else scrolls past. That is intended.*

17. If getting out of bed feels like climbing Everest in flip-flops, this one is for you.
18. You are functioning while exhausted and thinking clearly while carrying too much. I am not about to tell you to do less.
19. A smaller version of the thing still counts. That is not a consolation prize, it is the whole method.
20. Most people do not lose momentum because they failed. They lose it because they vanished quietly, and nobody called.

## 07 · The three phases

### Days 1 to 30 · Volume, and finding the signal

**The goal is not sales. The goal is to learn which three hooks and which pillar the
algorithm rewards.**

- Twenty videos out, all four platforms, no exceptions and no skipped days.
- Judge nothing before it has been live seven days. Short-form has a long tail and
  early numbers lie.
- Track saves per thousand views above every other metric. For a book, saves
  predict sales far better than likes do.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. It is a ranking input and it is free.
- Expect the numbers to be bad. Week three has no champagne in it.

### Days 31 to 60 · Double down, then push rank

**Kill the bottom half. Re-shoot the winners. Then concentrate a month of demand into
five days.**

- Take the top three hooks and shoot each one four more ways: different opening
  frame, different pillar, different song, different length.
- Stop making anything in the losing pillar. You now have data, so stop guessing.
- Run the five day rank push in week 7 or 8. Every link points at Amazon, not the site.
- Email the list on day one of the push, not day three. Kindle at its lowest price
  for those five days, then straight back up.
- Concentration is the whole trick. Fifty sales in five days moves category rank.
  The same fifty over a month moves nothing.

### Days 61 to 90 · Turn one book into three

**Read-through is where the money is. A reader who finished book one buys book two at
a rate no cold viewer will ever match.**

- Start seeding *The But I Will Era* and *Your Body Is Not Your Soul* in the
  content, not just in the back matter.
- Amazon series page live, all three linked, so anyone who finishes book one is
  shown the next two.
- Push the sixty day app to every buyer. It buys you sixty more days of contact
  with someone who has already paid you once.
- Retire the hooks that are now tired and harvest a fresh set. The niche moves
  every quarter.

## 08 · The Amazon half the video skips

This is a day of work, done once, and it keeps paying. The live listing could not be
loaded when this was written, so treat every line as something to check rather than
something confirmed.

### Do this first

- [ ] **The seven backend keyword slots.** Use whole phrases, not single words, and
      never repeat anything already in the title. Candidates: *self help for people
      who hate self help*, *how to stop waiting and start*, *motivation when
      exhausted*, *small steps big change habits*, *books for burnout and grief*,
      *discipline over motivation*, *anti guru self development*.
- [ ] **Categories.** Three, and at least one narrow enough that you can
      realistically rank top twenty in it. KDP support can add categories beyond the
      default in most marketplaces if you ask.
- [ ] **A+ Content.** Free through KDP and badly underused. Three modules: the
      Trenches paragraph, the three book series strip, the songs.
- [ ] **The Look Inside sample.** The first ten per cent has to close the sale on its
      own. Confirm the Trenches opening falls inside it. If it does not, restructure
      the front matter until it does.
- [ ] **The series page.** All three books linked as a series so a finisher is shown
      the next one automatically.

### Then

- [ ] **Reviews, honestly.** The email list, the app users, and a direct ask in the
      back matter. Never buy them and never trade them, it gets listings pulled.
- [ ] **Formats.** If it is Kindle only, paperback and audiobook are both money left
      on the table. Audiobook especially, given fifteen chapter conversations and a
      music catalogue already exist.
- [ ] **Price.** KDP royalty jumps from 35 to 70 per cent at $2.99, so a 99c price
      costs roughly four times more than it looks. Test $4.99 against $6.99 and keep
      99c for the five day pushes only.

## 09 · What the publishing engine can and cannot do

The Wild Hearts content engine already carries a `momentum-series` brand profile
with the voice rules, the avoid list and the Australian English constraints. Its
platform adapters cover Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Bluesky and
Pinterest.

Two gaps matter here. There is no TikTok adapter and no YouTube adapter, which are
precisely the two platforms the method leans on. And every adapter posts text and
images, not video.

Recommendation: leave it alone. Building video upload into the engine is weeks of
work to save ten minutes a day. Film on the phone, post the four video platforms by
hand, and let the engine keep doing the text platforms it already does well.

## 10 · The weekly numbers

One sheet, filled in every Sunday. Ten minutes.

| Source | What to record | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Each platform | Posts, views, average watch per cent, saves, follows | Which pillar and which hook shape is working |
| Short links | Clicks by platform tag | Which platform sends buyers, not just viewers |
| Site | Sessions, email signups, direct sales | Whether the page converts, separate from whether the video worked |
| Amazon | Units, KENP pages read, rank in each category | Whether social output is reaching the listing at all |

In month one, one number carries more information than the rest combined: **saves
per thousand views**. A like is a reflex. A save is someone deciding they want this
again later, which for a book is the closest free signal to intent to buy that any
platform gives you.

## What could not be verified

The seven steps were read off the video's burned-in captions and then cross-checked
against an independent transcription of the audio, which agreed on all seven.
Network egress from the authoring session blocked Amazon and themomentumrule.com, so
the live listing (price, formats, categories, review count) and the current state of
the site are unchecked. Book details, series position and site premise come from
search results; the voice, themes, songs and sixty day programme come from the
`wild-hearts-publishing` and `momentum60-app` repositories. The software named around
1:50 is a caption guess in both transcriptions and should not be bought on anyone's
say-so until it has been seen written down. The 150,000 figure is the creator's own
claim, made in a TikTok, and there is no way to test it.
